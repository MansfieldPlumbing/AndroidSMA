# Optical and acoustic signal recipe mission

## What is valuable

These specimens contain Morse framing, sample decoding, acknowledgement flow, V.21 FSK encoding and decoding, channel definitions, and self-tests.

> # SIREN WARNING
>
> Do not compile the C#, preserve static-class vocabulary, or mix Android acquisition into the algorithm port. Do not accept a pretty waveform as correctness. Platform access and signal mathematics are separate proofs.

## Permitted work

Create fresh PS1 encoders and decoders over explicit arrays and typed records. Add Android light or audio membranes only after vector parity.

## Proof gate

Fixed vectors, randomized round trips, silence, truncation, noise, timing drift, malformed frames, and retained-receipt parity must pass.
